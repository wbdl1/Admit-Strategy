Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Admit Strategy is a small free beta. It collects only the information needed to arrange coaching, communicate with students and parents or guardians, and maintain the private progress portal.

Information collected

  • Student name, grade, student email, and parent or guardian email.
  • Booking details and a brief description of the schoolwork or study problem.
  • Professional coaching records such as tasks, weak points, session summaries, progress ratings, and parent updates.
  • Optional permission to receive occasional study tips and beta updates.

How it is used

The information is used to deliver the beta, arrange meetings, send necessary reminders, update the portal, provide parent visibility, and improve the coaching system. Marketing messages are optional and require a separate unchecked opt-in.

Student and parent visibility

Portal content is intended to be visible to both the student and the parent or guardian. Notes must remain minimal, professional, and relevant to coaching. Do not submit medical, financial, identification-document, password, or other highly sensitive information.

Private portal links

A portal link works like a private password. Do not forward it. Access can be revoked or the link can be rotated if it is shared accidentally.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in private Google services used by Admit Strategy. Records are kept only while needed for the beta, follow-up, safety, and reasonable recordkeeping. When participation ends, portal access should be revoked and records reviewed for deletion or anonymisation within 90 days unless they are still needed to resolve an issue or the parent or guardian asks for continued retention.

Access, correction, or deletion

A student or parent or guardian can request access, correction, portal-link rotation, or deletion by emailing ryanwbdl@gmail.com. Reasonable identity verification may be requested before changing or deleting a student’s records.

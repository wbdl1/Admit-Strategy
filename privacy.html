Privacy notice

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Admit Strategy collects only the information needed to provide the student workspace, arrange support, communicate with students and parents or guardians, and maintain the private progress portal.

Information collected

  • Student name, grade, student email, and parent or guardian email.
  • Booking details and a brief description of the schoolwork or study problem.
  • Workspace records such as plan level, classes, study blocks, review cards, weak points, assessment details, projects, evidence, linked study-material URLs, session summaries, progress ratings, and parent updates.
  • If deliberately linked, the private iCal feed URL and imported events from a dedicated academic or extracurricular calendar.
  • Optional permission to receive occasional study tips and Admit Strategy updates.

How it is used

The information is used to provide the portal and any agreed support, build the rule-based Today plan, arrange meetings, send necessary reminders, sync approved calendar events, provide parent visibility, and improve the system. Marketing messages are optional and require a separate unchecked opt-in.

Calendar connections

Only link a dedicated school or activities calendar, not a personal calendar. The current connection uses a private iCal feed from Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, or Apple Calendar; it does not request direct access to the student’s calendar account. The private feed URL is never returned to the portal and is deleted when the calendar is disconnected. Possible assessments detected from events require confirmation before they affect the Today plan. Imported event records can be reviewed for deletion through the normal privacy-request process.

Study materials

The current portal stores HTTPS links and short descriptions for school-approved materials. It does not upload the file itself, read linked files, or process them with AI. Do not link documents containing sensitive personal information or content the student is not allowed to share.

Student and parent visibility

Portal content is intended to be visible to both the student and the parent or guardian. Notes must remain minimal, professional, and relevant to coaching. Do not submit medical, financial, identification-document, password, or other highly sensitive information.

Private portal links

A portal link works like a private password. Do not forward it. Access can be revoked or the link can be rotated if it is shared accidentally.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in private Google services used by Admit Strategy. Records are kept only while needed to provide the portal, follow-up, safety, and reasonable recordkeeping. When participation ends, portal access should be revoked and records reviewed for deletion or anonymisation within 90 days unless they are still needed to resolve an issue or the parent or guardian asks for continued retention.

Plans and payments

The Free plan does not require a subscription payment. The Plus plan shown on the website is a product preview and cannot yet be purchased. Before paid billing launches, pricing, renewal, cancellation, and any service limits will be shown clearly before checkout.

Access, correction, or deletion

A student or parent or guardian can request access, correction, portal-link rotation, or deletion by emailing ryanwbdl@gmail.com. Reasonable identity verification may be requested before changing or deleting a student’s records.

FAQ

Quick answers for students and parents before starting.

Is this an admissions guarantee?

No. Admit Strategy does not guarantee admission, grades, or another outcome. It helps students build stronger systems, clearer direction, and better execution.

Who is this built for first?

Middle and high school students in Doha, especially American, IB, and AP students who want stronger study habits, planning, and extracurricular execution.

Is this tutoring?

No. Admit Strategy does not replace a qualified subject tutor. It is the execution layer: study systems, planning, accountability, weak-point diagnosis, and direction applied to real schoolwork.

What does Plus cost?

The planned launch price is $12/month or $96/year in USD. Plus is not available for purchase yet, and no payment is collected today.

Is Free genuinely useful?

Yes. Free includes the core manual workspace: Today plan, classes, Box systems, study blocks, summatives, projects, evidence, and calendar-feed connection.

How do portal accounts work?

After the diagnosis and guardian approval, Admit Strategy emails a private passwordless access link. Student emails are normalized and checked first, so an existing active workspace is reused instead of creating a duplicate. The page never reveals whether an email already has an account.

Can I sign in with Google?

Not yet. Google sign-in will only be added with a real OAuth client, verified redirect configuration, and narrow permissions. The current emailed private-link access is the live account method.

What can students edit?

Students can add and edit classes, study blocks, Box terms, summatives, materials, tasks, projects, weak points, and evidence. Coach ratings and protected feedback stay controlled.

How does calendar connection work?

The current portal accepts a private iCal feed from a dedicated school or activities calendar. Detected assessments require student confirmation. Direct Google or Microsoft account authorization is planned, not presented as live.

Do parents get visibility?

Yes, where agreed. Parent approval, private student data, and access, correction, or deletion requests remain part of the process.

Does it use AI?

No paid AI feature is active. The diagnosis and Today plan use transparent rules; linked materials are organized but are not summarized or processed by AI.

Where are testimonials and results?

Admit Strategy will only publish real feedback, progress examples, or screenshots with appropriate student and guardian permission. Nothing is invented.
